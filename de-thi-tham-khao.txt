========================================
Thoi gian: 90 phut | 40 cau | 10 diem (0.25d/cau)

  - Trong am (2 cau): tu 2 am tiet, 3 am tiet

Phan 2: TU VUNG - NGU PHAP - GIAO TIEP (3.0d) - Cau 5-16
  - Tu vung trong ngu canh, phrasal verbs, gioi tu, thi
  - Tinh huong giao tiep thuc te (2 cau hoi thoai)
  - Bien bao / dau hieu cong cong (2 cau)

Phan 3: DOC HIEU (3.0d) - Cau 17-28
  - Cloze test / Dien tu (6 cau: 17-22)

  - Word Form (6 cau: 29-34)
  - Dictionary/Contextual Insertions (2 cau: 35-36) [DANG MOI]
  - Sentence Transformation (4 cau: 37-40)

2. DE THI CHINH THUC 2024-2025
========================================

PART 1 - PHONETICS (Cau 1-4)
Q1. Phat am duoi -s/-es: A. thinks B. looks C. predicts D. helps
    Dap an: C (predicts - /z/, cac tu con lai /s/)
Q2. Phat am nguyen am: A. campus B. campus C. visit D. official
    Dap an: B (campus - /æ/, cac tu con lai /ə/)
Q3. Trong am 2 am tiet: A. visit B. visits C. official D. could go
    Dap an: C
Q4. Trong am 3 am tiet: A. official B. campus C. visit D. predict
    Dap an: A

PART 2 - VOCABULARY, GRAMMAR & COMMUNICATION (Cau 5-16)
Q5. I wish I ___ enough money to buy a new laptop.
    A. have B. had C. has D. having
    Dap an: B
Q6. The children were excited ___ the trip to the zoo.
    A. about B. to C. in D. on
    Dap an: A
Q7. She suggested ___ to the countryside this weekend.
    A. go B. to go C. going D. went
    Dap an: C
Q8. The book ____ I read yesterday was very interesting.
    A. who B. whom C. which D. whose
    Dap an: C
Q9. He works very hard, ___ he?
    A. does B. is C. doesn't D. aren't we
    Dap an: D (Tag question: Let's... shall we? / Aren't we?)
Q10. Would you mind ___ the window?
     A. open B. to open C. opening D. opened
     Dap an: C
Q11. The doctor advised me ___ more exercise.
     A. taking B. take C. to be taken D. to take
     Dap an: D
Q12. She has been a teacher ___ 2015.
     A. since B. for C. in D. from
     Dap an: A
Q13. Student A: "I've passed the entrance exam!" Student B: "___"
     A. That's a good idea. B. Congratulations! C. Thank you. D. Not at all.
     Dap an: B
Q14. Student: "Would you mind if I used your phone?" Librarian: "___"
     A. Yes, I'd love to. B. Of course not. C. You're welcome. D. Never mind.
     Dap an: B
Q15. Sign: Hinh ve thung rac & toilet → Y nghia?
     C. Don't throw any kind of trash into the toilet
Q16. Sign: Bien bao nha hang PR → Y nghia?
     A. You have to book in advance to eat at PR

7. CAC DANG CAU HOI THEO PHAN
========================================

--- PHAN 1: NGU AM ---
* Phat am -ed: /t/, /d/, /ɪd/
* Phat am -s/-es: /s/, /z/, /ɪz/
* Phat am nguyen am: /æ/, /ɑː/, /e/, /iː/, /ɪ/, /ʌ/, /əʊ/, /aʊ/, /ɔː/, /ʊ/
* Trong am 2 am tiet: Dong tu (nhan am 2), Danh tu (nhan am 1)
* Trong am 3 am tiet: Quy tac hau to -tion, -sion, -ic, -ity, -graphy...

--- PHAN 2: TU VUNG - NGU PHAP - GIAO TIEP ---
* Thi: HTD, HTTD, HTHT, QKD, QKTD, TLG, TLD
* Cau dieu kien: Loai 1, 2, 3
* Cau bi dong: cac thi, dong tu khuyet thieu
* Cau tuong thuat: Reported speech
* Menh de quan he: who, whom, which, that, whose
* Phrasal verbs: give up, look after, turn off/on, put on, take off...
* Gioi tu: in, on, at, for, with, about, from, to...
* Cau giao tiep: chao hoi, xin phep, loi khuyen, de nghi, chuc mung
* Bien bao: cam, canh bao, huong dan, bat buoc...

--- PHAN 3: DOC HIEU ---
* Cloze test: Dien tu vao doan van 80-100 tu
  - Dien dai tu quan he, gioi tu, tu vung, lien tu
* Reading passage: 180-200 tu
  - True/False/Not Given
  - Multiple choice (main idea, detail, inference, vocabulary)

--- PHAN 4: VIET ---
* Word Form: danh tu, dong tu, tinh tu, trang tu, tien to/hau to
  - -tion/-sion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -al, -ous, -ful, -less, -ly, -ize/-ise,
    un-/in-/im-/ir-/dis-, re-, pre-, over-, under-
* Dictionary Insertion (Moi): Tra cuu tu dien, viet cum tu phu hop
* Sentence Transformation: Wish, If, Passive, Reported, Comparison...
